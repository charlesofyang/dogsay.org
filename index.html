🌍 Our Mission

What DogSay
Foundation does

We build the world's first verified dog language dataset using a community-powered platform, rigorous science, and the cooperation of dogs and their owners worldwide.

🔬
Rigorous Science
Every bark is verified by four independent signals before entering our dataset. We never guess. We never assume. The dog's own behaviour is the ground truth.
🌍
Community Powered
Dog owners worldwide contribute daily sessions. Their dogs are the scientists. Their observations are the data. The community builds the science together.
🐕
Dog First
Every feature serves the dog first. Personalised shows. Social connections. A world built for them. Their language decoded for their benefit and their owners' peace of mind.
⚙️ The Platform

How DogSay works

A game that dogs love. A show built for each dog. A scientific instrument that listens to every bark. All in one daily session.

1
Dog watches the show
Animated stimuli — bones, toys, friendly dogs, and threats — trigger genuine involuntary reactions. The screen shows their name. Their owner's voice plays. Their world appears.
2
Every bark is captured
Five precise capture windows record only the meaningful moments. Background noise filtered. Only the intentional vocalization — the "this one!" bark — is kept.
3
Meaning is verified
The dog's subsequent behaviour confirms what they said. Walks to bowl = food desire verified. Goes to door = outside desire verified. The dog verifies its own language.
4
Language grows daily
Verified vocabulary enters a personal dictionary for each dog and a universal model across all dogs. Every session teaches us more. Every dog makes every other dog easier to understand.

🧬 The Science

The progressive
learning cycle

The dog teaches the system its own language. The system teaches it back. A closed loop that improves with every single session.

1
Identify Acoustic analysis
Detect vocalization. Measure pitch, rhythm, intensity, duration, harmonic structure.
2
Infer meaning AI inference
Propose meaning based on game context, individual history, and universal patterns.
3
Verify by action Ground truth
Dog's behaviour confirms or rejects the proposed meaning. No human interpretation.
4
Build sound base Personal model
Verified vocabulary enters this dog's personal dictionary with full provenance.
5
Speak back Two-way
System synthesises the dog's own sounds and communicates back during the show.
6
Feed common base Universal model
Confirmed vocabulary shared with universal model. Every dog helps every other dog.
